Create ONE finished 16:9 landscape concept keyframe for a sophisticated typographic music video, with the exact English lyric “So I can see you again”. No 3D shading, gradients on the black props, metallic highlights, internal sketching, white outline, or cartoon eyes.

Two distinct LARGE shapes occupy the central-right part of the composition, CLOSE TOGETHER in an intimate arrangement, NOT stranded at opposite edges. Typography occupies the upper-left and middle-left negative space, with balanced asymmetry and ample breathing room. The EXACT lyric is split naturally into “So I can”, the larger word “see”, then “you again”. No handwriting, no calligraphy, no wild swashes, no gothic/blackletter, no faux cursive, no extreme slant. Typography feels part of the scene, not a subtitle bar across the top; it should have strong presence while allowing the interacting silhouettes to dominate the center. The visual mood is tenderness, stillness and impending danger. Broad simple black masses, disciplined negative space, editorial precision, atmospheric without extra decorations.
